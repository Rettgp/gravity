Gravity Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Gravity is a private dashboard used by a single family. It is not a public service: only people whose email address is on the family's allowlist can sign in, and nobody else can create an account. This policy explains what Gravity collects, how it is used, and how to have it deleted.

Information we collect

  • Google sign-in details. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and a unique account identifier. We use these only to check that you are on the family allowlist and to show your name in the app.
  • Journal entries you create. Meals, symptoms, notes, and which days you felt unwell, along with the family profiles you set up.
  • Health and fitness data you choose to connect. If you connect your Google Health (Fitbit) account, Gravity reads activity and fitness data (such as steps and active minutes), health metrics (such as heart rate, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, oxygen saturation, skin temperature and breathing rate), and sleep data. If you connect an iPhone, Gravity receives the Apple Health data you choose to send. Nothing is collected until you connect a source yourself.

How we use it

Your data is used only to run Gravity for your family: showing your journal and health trends, and calculating personal insights such as how sleep, heart rate variability or foods relate to the days you felt unwell. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to build profiles for anyone else.

Google user data and Limited Use

Gravity's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, data received from the Google Health API:

  • is used only to show you your own health trends and journal insights inside Gravity;
  • is never transferred to anyone else, except as needed to store it (see below) or when required by law;
  • is never used for advertising, and never sold;
  • is never read by a person, except with your permission, for security reasons, or when required by law;
  • is never used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Data is stored in the family's own Amazon Web Services account (United States, Ohio region), encrypted in transit and at rest. Every request must carry a valid sign-in from an allowlisted family member. Journal entries and health data belong to a profile and are visible only to the people who manage that profile. You can choose to share individual journal days with the rest of the family. Health data is never included in shared days.

Gravity does not use advertising or analytics trackers and does not share data with third parties. The only outside services involved are Google (for sign-in and, if you connect it, Google Health) and Amazon Web Services (for hosting).

Keeping and deleting your data

  • You can edit or delete any journal day at any time in the app.
  • You can disconnect Google Health in Gravity, which stops all further access and deletes the imported health data. You can also remove Gravity's access at any time from your Google Account permissions.
  • If you are removed from the family allowlist you can no longer sign in. You can ask for all of your data to be deleted by contacting the family member who invited you, or the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.

Children

Parents may keep journal profiles for their children. Children do not sign in themselves, and their profiles are visible only to the parents who manage them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
